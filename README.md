# Bekal

Learning records refugees carry themselves. An idea for the **Idea Track** of *From Vision to Impact: Building Web3 for Social Good 2026* (SMU, GSR Foundation, UMA on DoraHacks).

*Bekal* is Indonesian for the provisions you take on a journey: here, proof of what a refugee has learned, held by the learner, checkable by any school, scholarship or employer, and never written to a blockchain about them.

- [`docs/PROPOSAL.md`](docs/PROPOSAL.md): the idea, as judged (challenge, users, solution, why Web3, impact, feasibility), with sources.
- [`docs/SUBMISSION.md`](docs/SUBMISSION.md): the DoraHacks fields, ready to paste.
- [`logo.svg`](logo.svg), `logo.png`: the BUIDL logo.

Status: proposal only. The Idea Track does not need a prototype at submission; shortlisted ideas get AI-assisted development support before the final on 26 November 2026.

Separate from [Naungan](https://github.com/PugarHuda/naungan) (Tech Track, BUIDL 50178), by the same author.
